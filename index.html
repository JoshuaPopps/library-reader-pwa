Get the manuals

This reader ships with no books in it — the manuals are copyrighted, so get your own copy and load the PDF here. Where to buy:

  • Factory Harley manuals — your Harley-Davidson dealer, or used from The Knee Slider (1997–98 Softail manual 99482-98 listed at $115). Harley's own Service Information Portal is a subscription that covers 2001 and newer models — it won't have the Evo books.
  • Clymer Evo manual (1984–1999) — new from Haynes (print + online bundle $52.76), or used on eBay (from about $30.80).
  • Easyriders Tech Tips & Tricks — out of print, used copies on ThriftBooks (Vol. 3, $53.39) or eBay ($54.95 or best offer).

Prices checked November 25, 2026 — they move, so check the listing before you buy.

Your data

No account, no server, no sync. Your books live in this browser's storage — the PDFs in IndexedDB, the titles, bookmarks, and notes in local storage. Back them up with Export.

Export

The backup is metadata only — book titles, bookmarks, and notes. The PDFs themselves are not in it; load them again on a new device, then import, and the bookmarks find their books again.

Import

Import merges bookmarks and notes into this library. A bookmark or note is applied only when a book with the same title and size is already here; the rest are skipped and named, so you can load those PDFs first and import again. Import never deletes anything and never adds books.

Clear the library

Deletes every book, its PDF, and all bookmarks and notes on this device. The shared bike roster is not touched — this app does not use it.
